Phone alerts

Install the free ntfy app on your phone (App Store or Google Play), then subscribe to the topic below. Rules with “Send an alert to my phone” ticked will ring it.

The topic is an unguessable name on the public ntfy.sh server — no account needed. Alerts carry one snapshot of the moment; run your own ntfy and put its address in “Alert service” to keep even that inside your house.

Home automation

Porchlight can announce every event on an MQTT broker, so Home Assistant (or anything else on your network) can react to it. Leave the broker empty to keep this off.

Events arrive on topic/event/<camera> and camera health on topic/camera/<camera>/status.
